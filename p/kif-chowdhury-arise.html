The window

Most athletes earn more in 10 years than most people earn in 30.

The problem isn't the income. It's what doesn't get built during it. Sport gives you a window most people never get — but it closes faster than it opens. And sometimes it closes without warning.

When the career ends on your terms

Retirement, a move overseas, life after sport — whatever it looks like. The athletes who land well aren't the highest earners. They're the ones who had a structure running quietly in the background for years before that day came. Property bought smart, compounding, rented and working.

When it doesn't

A serious injury. A contract not renewed. Something that changes the plan overnight. These things happen — and when they do, what matters is what was already in place. Structure set up. Equity building. Assets that don't depend on the career continuing to keep working.
