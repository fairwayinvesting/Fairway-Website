The process

A clear path from
strategy to settlement.

01
Getting started

Onboarding Questionnaire

Before anything else, we need to understand you. Your financial position, investment goals, risk appetite, existing assets, and preferences. The questionnaire is completed through your private client portal and gives us everything we need to build a strategy that actually fits your situation — not a generic template.

02
Strategy

Your Buying Brief

Based on your questionnaire, we build a personalised Buying Brief — a strategic document that defines your target markets, budget, property criteria, acquisition strategy, and the specific things we want to avoid. You'll have full visibility and input before we begin searching. Nothing moves until we're aligned on the brief.

03
Research

Market Research Reports

We assign you dedicated research reports on your target markets — all available through your client portal. These cover median prices, rental yields, growth trends, days on market, supply and demand dynamics, and more. You'll understand exactly why a market is on your brief before we pursue a single property in it.

04
Communication

Dedicated WhatsApp Group

A private WhatsApp group is created for us to communicate throughout the engagement. You'll get real-time property alerts, updates, quick answers, and progress notes — no delays, no email threads.

05
The main event

Property Acquisition

This is where we get to work. Every property we present has already passed our internal filter — you'll only ever see the ones worth your attention. From first shortlist to settlement day, we manage everything on your behalf.

Sourcing
We identify, filter, and shortlist properties that match your brief — leveraging agent relationships, off-market channels, and market data.
Due Diligence
Full analysis on every property we recommend — building & pest, title search, comparable sales, rental history, and risk assessment.
Negotiations
We negotiate on your behalf with no emotion and no agenda other than your result — using market data and local agent relationships to secure the best possible terms.
Professional Network
We coordinate your solicitor, building & pest inspector, and introduce you to a vetted local property manager — everything in place from settlement day one.
