Step 05 — The main event

Property acquisition,
end to end.

Sourcing

We identify, filter, and shortlist properties matching your brief — leveraging agent relationships, off-market channels, and live market data. You only see the ones worth your attention.

Due Diligence

Full analysis on every property we present — building & pest inspection, title search, comparable sales, rental history, council checks, and risk assessment.

Negotiations

We negotiate on your behalf with no emotion and no agenda other than your result — using market data, comparable sales, and agent relationships to secure the best possible terms.

Professional Network

We coordinate your solicitor and building & pest inspector, and introduce you to a vetted local property manager — everything in place from settlement day one.
