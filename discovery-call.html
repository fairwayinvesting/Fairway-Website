What to expect

How the call works

1

Your situation

I'll ask about where you are right now — financially, professionally, and in terms of what you actually want out of property. There are no wrong answers, and nothing you say will be used to pressure you into anything.

2

The strategy

Based on what you share, I'll map out what a realistic path looks like for you — the kind of property, the right market, a rough timeline, and what stands between you and getting it done.

3

Is this the right fit?

I'll be direct about whether I think Fairway is the right partner for where you are. If the timing's not right, or I don't think I can genuinely help you, I'll say so — and point you in the right direction instead.

No surprises

What this call isn’t

  • × A property presentation. I won’t be pitching listings or showing you deals on the call.
  • × A high-pressure sales call. I work with a small number of clients and I’m selective. I’d rather turn someone away than take on a bad fit.
  • × An information dump. You won’t leave with a 40-page report. You’ll leave with clarity on your next step.
  • × A commitment to anything. No obligation. No follow-up pressure. No contract to sign.

It’s a genuine conversation. I’ll ask the right questions, listen properly, and give you my honest read on your situation — whether that benefits me or not.
