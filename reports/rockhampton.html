Rockhampton is the administrative and commercial capital of Central Queensland, 650km north of Brisbane on the Fitzroy River. Known as Australia's Beef Capital, the city is the hub of one of the world's largest cattle industries, while also serving as the regional centre for government services, healthcare, education and retail across a vast central Queensland catchment. CQUniversity's main campus is headquartered in Rockhampton, and the city hosts the region's principal public hospital.

Rockhampton's economy is anchored by beef production and processing, government services, healthcare and education. CQUniversity — headquartered in Rockhampton — is one of Australia's largest universities by student numbers, creating a consistent renter base. The city has historically offered some of Australia's highest gross rental yields due to its affordability and the income levels generated by the surrounding agricultural and resources economy.

The $750M+ Rockhampton Base Hospital expansion is the single largest infrastructure commitment in the city's history — it will materially expand healthcare employment and attract specialist medical professionals to the region on a permanent basis, creating a new high-quality renter cohort in a market that already offers exceptional yield.

Comparable market precedent

Rockhampton's income-first market profile — high yield, stable essential services employment, affordable entry — shares characteristics with markets such as Dubbo and Toowoomba at earlier stages of their infrastructure investment cycles. Past performance in those markets is not a guide to future performance in Rockhampton.

Relative affordability

At ~$370k median, Rockhampton offers the highest indicative gross yield profile of any market in this report at this entry point. The combination of an essential services employer base, a major university and a committed hospital expansion creates genuine, structural rental demand at a price level that most east-coast investors would find difficult to match anywhere close to their home market.

What to watch

Risks & conditions to monitor

Beef industry exposure

A significant portion of Rockhampton's economy is linked to cattle production and processing. Prolonged drought, disease events or market disruptions can reduce agricultural income across the region, affecting employment and spending — though the hospital and university employment base provides a structural floor.

Flood risk

Rockhampton has historically experienced flooding along the Fitzroy River. Properties in flood-prone areas require careful due diligence, appropriate insurance, and may be subject to land-use restrictions. Property selection and local due diligence are critical to managing this risk.

Capital growth ceiling

At current price levels, capital growth will be driven primarily by infrastructure investment and employment growth rather than affordability arbitrage. Investors should calibrate expectations — this is primarily an income market with secondary growth characteristics driven by the hospital and university investment pipeline.

Fairway's view

Luke's take

Luke Clifford

Founder, Fairway Investing

Rockhampton is the clearest income-first market in our current research coverage. The yield profile is exceptional at current prices, and the employment base — hospital, university, government, beef industry — is about as diversified and resilient as you'll find in a city of this size. The $750M+ hospital expansion is a genuine game-changer for the quality of the local renter base.

I look for properties here that serve the professional and healthcare renter base — not because one tenant type is better than another, but because professional renters tend to have longer tenancies and more predictable rental behaviour. The right property, in a location that appeals to that profile, delivers a very strong income position that is genuinely hard to replicate at this price point anywhere else in Australia.
